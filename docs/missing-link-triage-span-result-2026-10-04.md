# Luna span comparison: better provenance, unresolved semantic errors

The reviewed nine-case comparison completed with **nine terminal responses and
nine mechanically valid cards**, without retries. The two previously unsupported
outcome slices no longer appear: external anchoring becomes unknown, and hosted
help becomes different. The explicitly requested average retains a bounded slice.
All nine cards remain `context_required`; **no lead is qualified**.

This is not a production-promotion result. Independent reading of all 22 known
facets finds runtime/behavior confusion, unsupported wrapper-interface claims,
broader-project comparisons and a wrong selected demand chunk. Valid IDs establish
original-text provenance, not support for those interpretations.

The model did not receive prior predictions or human case labels. The new prompt

autonomous discovery or evidence of full-pool ranking improvement.

compression study requests reuse of its existing compressor and exact reversible
codec/evaluation work, while only this encoding half is selected. Earlier human
primitive labels were retrospective references, not adoption proof. A new review
must not force them back to slice just to match those labels.
