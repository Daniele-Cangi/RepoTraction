## Assessment criteria fixed before execution

Review every returned facet's reason against the original demand and selected
source body, including unknown facets. Keep three assessments separate:

1. Mechanical acceptance: terminal status, schema, original IDs/provenance,
   bounded declarations and completeness guards. This does not certify truth.
2. Source accuracy: whether each explanation and property is supported at its
   named layer. Record unsupported claims, missing evidence and unassessable
   context explicitly. An unknown relation with a false reason is not a success.
3. Bounded usefulness: whether a supported local comparison helps understand
   the request. A known-facet count is not a quality score or a qualified lead.

For case 1, examine both input and output reasons. The selected factory receives
a copy callback and returns a callable; the returned callable later takes paths,
conditionally returns the destination, or returns an unseen copy delegate's
result. A narrow abstention about that unseen contract is acceptable. Attributing
the later path arguments/result to the factory is not. Do not force known facets
merely to eliminate the old explanation defect.

For case 6, inspect the visible conversion, encoded timestamp, separator and
signature composition while preserving the limits of unseen helpers. Helper
names do not prove clock/network policy or a full external-proof workflow. Do not
replace the original demand with the authored fixture's stricter direct-step
requirement. A known local mechanism contrast is optional and needs its own
support; absence of the historical timestamp label is not automatically wrong.

Across the other cases, check the command-versus-boolean contrasts, broad
acquisition gaps, declared versus actual conditional/pass-through behavior,
unseen serialization/grouping delegates, bisector factory/method layers and
the bounded mean slice. Do not certify universal return shapes from one branch,
infer unsupported runtime bans or turn missing wiring into incompatibility.

The prior seven known facets and 29 abstentions are observations, not gold labels.
An improved case 1 explanation cannot compensate for new unsupported known claims
elsewhere. Publish per-case source-based findings and limits before another prompt
revision. One response per familiar case permits a descriptive comparison, not a
causal improvement claim, accuracy percentage or evidence of autonomous usefulness.
No automatic classifier scores unknown-reason truth. No positive lead is forced.
