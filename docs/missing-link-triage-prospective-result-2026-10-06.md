Five facets need correction for mechanism-grounded triage, independently of their
valid schemas and resolving citations:

  aligned to list-valued chunk output based on repeated documentation. The

  abstention is defensible, but omits the separately visible conditional strict
  length check as a possible bounded contribution; this does not force a slice.
- **Case 3 output and outcome:** the None-specific scope is careful, but the
  [visible `Markup()` call](https://github.com/pallets/markupsafe/blob/4e111271b1494995708a9e878a10829b5e10c7ba/src/markupsafe/__init__.py#L48)
  does not establish empty contents without the unseen constructor's contract.
  Repeating the docstring example is declared behavior, not body demonstration.
  The input's explicit None branch remains a supported narrower observation.
- **Case 5 output and outcome:** the
  [conditional separator translation](https://github.com/python-humanize/humanize/blob/6ed367fbeee9bfc4250789b5e47b1011c99a46b6/src/humanize/number.py#L143)
  is visible, but helper values are absent. A potential conditional policy does
  not establish an actual counterexample to comma output. Not proving comma
  behavior unconditionally is not proof of different behavior. The formatting
  composition remains a possible bounded contribution, not a forced slice.

These findings do not claim the upstream libraries malfunction: they concern what
the supplied evidence supports and the strength of the returned labels. Honest
qualifications in reasons help, but do not turn unsupported known declarations
into verified behavior. Local acceptance is intentionally not semantic certification.
