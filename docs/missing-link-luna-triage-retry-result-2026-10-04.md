The useful observations are bounded. NumPy's mixed-array preservation request is
not fulfilled by scalar text-to-bytes conversion. D3's mean supplies only the
average component of Timeline's broader profiling request. The compression
method supplies a conditional byte transformation, not a decoder, a native
model-token study or measured savings. The traversal wrapper supplies generator
iteration and a follow callback, not POSIX policy correctness or a complete
filesystem workstream. None of these observations establishes adoptability.

The `group` wrapper result is appropriately conservative: delegation does not
establish unseen helper behavior, generated key-tree output or performance. The
Hermes outcome abstention is also defensible under this narrower operation
context: the wrapper delegates copying and does not establish failure-safe
publication. Its difference from the reference is not, by itself, a model error;
its input quotation nevertheless fails the explicit citation contract.

Two raw slice hints are not established contributions to the cited demand:

- Locally attaching a timestamp and signature does not supply an independently
  controlled external anchor. Luna explicitly acknowledges this missing property,
  yet labels the outcome `slice`. This is a partial-value overclaim under the
  reference interpretation; exact source provenance does not cure it.
- Sorted-array insertion/boundary lookup is only hypothetical help-search support
  without evidence that the requested TOC or search representation uses it.
  Luna acknowledges the missing representation but still returns `slice`.
  All four quotations in this response also fail; the raw card remains invalid.

Other facet labels need caution. In particular, a main-only CI step versus a
Python method is not evidence of a runtime conflict: CI may execute Python. The
response disclaims impossible interoperation, but the known `different` label
still risks confusing missing integration with a demonstrated runtime difference.
Several input/output facets compare a whole project artifact with a primitive's
interface; those are scope differences, not automatically adoption conflicts.

This reading is a separate human assessment of preserved output. It neither
changes the raw cards nor upgrades or downgrades historical stored comparisons.
