## Why the mean card is rejected

The provider schema permits string-valued properties, including empty strings.
The existing local contract separately requires a known relation, including
`slice`, to supply both nonempty bounded property declarations. Case 9 retained
the requested-operation scope, entrypoint layer, citation IDs and three slice
description fields, but omitted the two required properties. The exact error is
`Known relation needs bounded concrete property declarations`.

The local guard worked: it did not infer or fill those declarations from the
slice description, certify the card, retry the model or drop the raw failure.
An apparently useful text explanation is not equivalent to a usable contract
result. This is a model contract-compliance failure, not a transport failure or
an accepted mean slice preserved from the previous run.
