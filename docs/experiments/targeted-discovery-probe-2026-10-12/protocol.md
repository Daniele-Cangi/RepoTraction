## Assessment and stopping rule

Measure inquiry relevance, acquired implementation, decision changes, searches,
selected sources, calls, reservations/usage and elapsed time separately. A useful
result requires independently justified demand, source-owned contribution, native
target integration/acceptance and a concrete advantage over the strongest direct
alternative on common inputs. Partial useful work may count if its bounded benefit
is demonstrated; fluent explanations, richer context or more candidates do not.
Any integration is assessed against the frozen criteria without changing the
discovery output or supplying a replacement donor. Unknown remains unknown.

If no such progress appears, close this one experiment. Do not rescue the cohort
with more prompts, file selectors, weights or expanded limits. The next research
direction is the separately discussed assistance-to-agent variant; unused budget
does not authorize that experiment. The negative result does not prove this
strategy impossible. Existing examples remain regression controls, not held-out
evidence. Test mechanics offline and preserve full CI for product modifications.
