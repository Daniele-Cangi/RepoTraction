Implementation rationale
This is compact batched Edmonds contraction, not a completed GGST implementation. An artificial root with penalty n*(max_weight-min_weight)+1 gives an exact integer reduction from unrestricted root choice to a rooted arborescence. Original weights are shifted by their minimum. A feasible optimum uses exactly one artificial edge; after expansion it is removed. Otherwise the existing no-spanning exception is raised. Each round chooses incoming edges and contracts all disjoint cycles together. Edge records carry original identities. Expansion stores chosen incoming edges plus original-vertex membership rather than retaining whole intermediate graphs. Output uses original endpoints and original attributes. This avoids NetworkX graph copying and mutation inside the contraction loop. It does not introduce a new public API or dependency, and does not claim a better generalized asymptotic bound than classical Edmonds.
