## Interpretation and decision

The native direct correction is demonstrated locally. Assistance effectiveness,
source-selection quality, conceptual transfer and the A/B comparison are
**unmeasured**: Missing Link never reached planning or search. This is not evidence
that mechanism transfer works or fails. Agent self-report and passing target
tests from the common attempt do not establish Missing Link benefit.

Stop this cohort as specified. Do not change the timeout, prompts, candidate pool
or acceptance to rescue the run, and do not expand the infrastructure in response.
This result warrants no product implementation. If a later authorized experiment
is chosen, the important functional question remains whether assistance improves
an unresolved, publicly grounded target need beyond the strongest direct solution;
the original public defect here already has a working direct correction. The two
generalizations alone do not establish practical demand for a donor mechanism.
